Non-standard automation equipment looks to the future
2022-05-18 Reading:6135

In the context of Automation Equipment Industry 4.0 and "Internet +", there has been a new reform in the future development and competition of CNC systems. More competition in China will focus on how to use the advantages of the Internet to infinitely expand the computing power of CNC systems, and through the understanding of emerging business models such as the sharing economy, rationally creating functions that are suitable for them will become an important trend in the future.

During the machining process, the machine tool uses various sensors and uses real-time monitoring and compensation technology to further improve the performance of the machine tool. Japanese companies such as Mazak and Okuma have provided many advanced technologies in terms of intelligence, such as spindle vibration suppression, intelligent anti-collision and other functions. Shenyang Machine Tool i5 CNC provides feature-based programming and graphical diagnosis and other functions.

The development of intelligence is a step-by-step process. Currently, there are different understandings of intelligence, and there is no universally applicable solution. The innovation and practical operation of CNC machine tool business models must rely on the intelligence and networking of CNC systems.

In the future, CNC systems will increasingly penetrate the influence of the Internet into the manufacturing process. Through the accumulation, transmission and mining of data, more and more intelligent manufacturing capabilities will be born. Transparency and sharing will bring about earth-shaking changes in the manufacturing industry.
