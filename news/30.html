My opinion on the non-standard automation industry
2022-05-18 Reading:6779

Let’s start with competition. There is competition in all walks of life. Competition in the non-standard automation industry is different from other industries. It is an all-round and three-dimensional competition. First, talent competition: Since companies in the non-standard automation industry do not have core technologies, only core technical personnel, many people may not agree with this statement, especially business owners. Don’t believe it, and please respect the core technical personnel. Some unscrupulous companies maliciously poach people, ignoring the industry wage level, and poaching people with double salary at every turn. The consequences are that cats and dogs all enjoy high salaries, causing technical personnel in the industry to be unable to study technology with peace of mind, and are considering frequently changing jobs to increase their worth. In particular, some so-called headhunters in the industry ignore the interests of customers and the prospects of technical personnel. They are crazy about pimping and digging in and out of the company, causing people to panic. Second, project competition: This is the most important competition, and it is also the most intense competition. In the competition, it is life and death. Business, plan, and quotation are indispensable. I don’t understand business methods. This is a sales technology and will not be discussed for now. The plan is actually to tell stories. In the earliest days of the non-standard automation industry, there was no such position for storytellers. As business volume increases and projects become more complex, more and more non-standard automation companies are equipped with excellent storytellers. Storytelling is not about fooling customers. The manufacturing of non-standard automation equipment itself is the process of turning stories into reality. The quality of storytelling is a reflection of the level of professionalism. Therefore, even if the technology is not good enough, you still need to learn to tell stories. Quotation is the continuation of storytelling, which is the embodiment of the value of the story. It is not recommended to blindly increase the quotation for the sake of profit. You cannot treat customers as fools, nor do you advocate lowering quotations without limit for the sake of orders. You cannot treat competitors as fools. You may be successful for a while, but in this industry, whoever dares to accept projects below the cost will die first. The non-standard automation industry cannot tolerate manufacturers with no bottom line, so everyone should take care of themselves.

Let’s talk about the prospects. With the advancement of science and technology and the improvement of living standards, all walks of life have more and more demands for automation. It seems that the non-standard automation industry has good prospects. Precisely because the word "non-standard" may not have such a good future, let me tell you: non-standard automation developed from the auto parts industry. Now many manufacturers' main business also comes from the auto parts industry. With the great development of my country's auto industry, everyone is doing well, and new automation companies are born every day. However, with the saturation of automobiles, the industry seems to be very depressed recently, and many manufacturers are facing insufficient business volume. In fact, this is the biggest problem in the non-standard automation industry. Non-standard means that there is customer demand, which cannot be planned by anyone. To put it bluntly, it means "you have eaten the last meal but not the next meal". I'm not being alarmist. No matter how many projects you are currently working on or discussing, only what is in the basket is the food. At the same time, it is not enough for this industry to only focus on developing its own quality. It must have the cooperation of professional and high-quality customers. Otherwise, the profits of the project will gradually be lost due to the unprofessionalism of the customers. We cannot expect Party A's customers to fully cooperate with our work, but we must discuss issues on at least one channel and not make unreasonable troubles. I won't go into details because I'm afraid of offending customers. Recently, the popular Science and Technology Innovation Board has arrived. Have you noticed that many listed companies are from the automation industry, but they are not strictly non-standard automation. Regardless of the industry they are engaged in, they all have a common feature: the equipment they design and manufacture can be copied, and profits come from increases in volume, not technological breakthroughs. Every project in the non-standard automation industry is a challenge. After crossing the mountains and the sea, we have gained something, whether it is customer satisfaction or technological improvement. But what is the point? The scale cannot be scaled up at all. No securities companies are interested in it, let alone packaging. This is also the reason why non-standard automation companies do not need to be listed, and there is no chance to go public. To sum up, the way out for non-standard automation is to transform non-standard automation into standard automation. Don’t ignore previous development because of non-standard. Only replicable technology and R&D can create more value and opportunities. Don’t be overly obsessed with non-standard development. After a certain foundation, both technicians and bosses need to realize their previous efforts, rather than dedicating themselves to the non-standard automation industry all their lives.
